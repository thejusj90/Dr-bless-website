Bad breath in Goa

Persistent bad breath can have oral causes

Bad breath can be temporary, but when it persists despite normal brushing it may be useful to evaluate the teeth, tongue, gums, oral hygiene and saliva. Some causes may also be outside the mouth.

When should a dentist check it?If bad breath is persistent, especially with bleeding gums, swelling, loose teeth, visible deposits or an unpleasant taste, a dental and gum evaluation can help identify oral factors.

Oral factors a dentist may assess

Plaque and calculus

Build-up around teeth and gums can contribute to inflammation and odour.

Gum health

Periodontal problems can be associated with persistent bad breath and may require more than routine cleaning.

Dry mouth and hygiene

Reduced saliva, tongue coating and difficulty cleaning certain areas may contribute to persistent odour.

What if the mouth looks healthy?

Not all persistent bad breath originates from dental disease. If an oral examination does not identify a likely dental cause, the dentist may advise discussing other possible causes with an appropriate medical professional.
