Bleeding gums in Goa

Why do my gums bleed?

Occasional irritation can happen, but repeated gum bleeding should not simply be ignored. It may be associated with plaque-related inflammation, gum disease, brushing trauma or other factors that need individual assessment.

When should I see a dentist?If your gums bleed repeatedly, are swollen or tender, or you notice recession, bad breath or loose teeth, a dental or periodontal evaluation is useful.

Common things a dentist may check

Plaque and calculus

Build-up around the gum line can contribute to inflammation and bleeding.

Gum condition

Redness, swelling, recession and periodontal measurements can help show whether deeper gum problems are present.

Home-care habits

Brushing technique, interdental cleaning and recent changes in oral hygiene may be relevant.

Will a cleaning fix bleeding gums?

Sometimes routine cleaning and improved home care are enough when inflammation is mild. If periodontal disease is present, deeper or more structured gum treatment may be needed. The difference can only be determined after examination.
