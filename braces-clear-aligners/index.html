Braces & clear aligners in Goa

Orthodontic options for alignment and bite

Braces and clear aligners are used to move teeth gradually. The right option depends on tooth position, bite, oral health and the complexity of the movement required.

Which is better: braces or aligners?There is no single best option for everyone. A clinical assessment is needed to decide whether braces, clear aligners or another approach is suitable.

Reasons people seek an orthodontic consultation

Crowded teeth

Teeth that overlap or do not have enough space may be assessed for orthodontic movement.

Spacing

Gaps between teeth may have different causes and can sometimes be managed orthodontically.

Bite concerns

The relationship between upper and lower teeth is evaluated as part of treatment planning.

What an orthodontic assessment may include

  • Examination of tooth alignment and bite.
  • Assessment of gum and tooth health before movement begins.
  • Photographs, scans or dental imaging when required.
  • Discussion of appliance options, expected duration and maintenance.

Oral hygiene during orthodontic treatment

Keeping teeth and gums clean is particularly important during orthodontic treatment. Brackets and other appliances can create additional plaque-retentive areas, while aligners also require consistent cleaning and wear habits.

Frequently asked questions

Can adults get braces or clear aligners?

Adults can be assessed for orthodontic treatment. Suitability depends on oral health, tooth movement required and other clinical factors.

How long does treatment take?

Duration varies significantly according to the starting position of the teeth, the treatment goals and the appliance used.

Do I need cleaning before braces?

Any active dental or gum problems may need attention before orthodontic treatment starts.
