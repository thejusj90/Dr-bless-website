Crowns and bridges in Goa

Dental crowns and bridges for damaged or missing teeth

Crowns and bridges are fixed restorations used in different situations. A crown covers and protects a tooth; a bridge can replace one or more missing teeth by using nearby support.

Which option do I need?That depends on the condition of the tooth, the surrounding teeth, your bite and how much healthy tooth structure remains.

When a crown may be considered

Heavily restored tooth

A tooth with a large filling or significant loss of structure may need additional protection.

After root canal treatment

Some root-canal-treated teeth may benefit from a crown, depending on the tooth and remaining structure.

Cracked or worn tooth

A crown may be considered when a tooth is weakened by fracture, wear or damage.

When a bridge may be considered

A bridge may be one option for replacing a missing tooth when the teeth beside the space can provide suitable support. Dental implants and removable options may also be discussed.

What the assessment may include

  • Examination of the tooth or missing-tooth area.
  • Assessment of neighbouring teeth and the bite.
  • Dental imaging when clinically required.
  • Discussion of materials, alternatives and maintenance.

Frequently asked questions

Is a crown always needed after a root canal?

No. The need for a crown depends on the tooth, its location and how much sound tooth structure remains.

Is a bridge the same as an implant?

No. A bridge is supported by teeth or other structures, while an implant is placed in the jaw to support a restoration.

How long do crowns and bridges last?

Longevity varies with material, bite, oral hygiene, habits and regular dental maintenance.
