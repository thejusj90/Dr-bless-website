Dental fillings in Goa

Dental fillings to restore decayed or damaged teeth

Dental fillings repair areas of tooth structure affected by decay, small fractures or wear. The material and size of the restoration depend on the tooth and clinical findings.

Does every cavity need the same filling?No. The depth, position, bite forces and amount of remaining tooth structure influence the treatment plan.

Reasons a filling may be recommended

Dental decay

A cavity may be restored after the decayed tooth structure is assessed and treated.

Chipped tooth

Small chips can sometimes be repaired with a direct restorative material.

Old filling problems

A worn, fractured or leaking restoration may need review and possible replacement.

What happens during the appointment

  • The tooth and symptoms are assessed.
  • Dental imaging may be advised if needed.
  • The damaged area is prepared when treatment is indicated.
  • A suitable restorative material is selected and the bite is checked.

Frequently asked questions

Can all cavities be treated with a filling?

No. Larger or deeper problems may require a different restoration or additional treatment.

Do tooth-coloured fillings match natural teeth?

Composite materials are available in different shades and can often be selected to blend with surrounding tooth structure.

Can a filling cause sensitivity?

Temporary sensitivity can occur after some restorations. Persistent or worsening symptoms should be reviewed.
