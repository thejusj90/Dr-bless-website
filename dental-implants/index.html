Dental implants for missing teeth

Dental implants are one option for replacing missing teeth. A personalised plan depends on the condition of the gums, available bone, bite and the number and position of missing teeth.

What happens first?An implant consultation begins with a clinical assessment. Dental imaging may be advised when required for treatment planning.

When implants may be considered

One missing tooth

An implant-supported crown may be considered as a replacement option in suitable cases.

Several missing teeth

Implant-supported restorations may be discussed when multiple teeth are missing.

Denture support

Some patients may be assessed for implant-supported solutions to improve denture stability.

Implant assessment

  • Review of your dental and medical history.
  • Examination of the teeth, gums and the missing-tooth area.
  • Assessment of available space, bite and surrounding structures.
  • Dental imaging when clinically required.
  • Discussion of alternatives, stages and maintenance.

Why gum health matters

Long-term implant care includes healthy gums, effective daily cleaning and professional maintenance. Dr. Blessin Mathew holds an MDS in Periodontics and Oral Implantology.

Frequently asked questions

Is everyone suitable for dental implants?

No. Suitability can only be decided after an individual clinical assessment.

Are there alternatives to implants?

Yes. Depending on the case, alternatives may include bridges, removable dentures or other treatment plans.

How long does implant treatment take?

Timelines vary according to the site, healing and whether additional procedures are required.
