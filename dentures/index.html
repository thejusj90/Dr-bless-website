Dentures in Goa

Removable options for missing teeth

Dentures can replace several missing teeth or a full arch of teeth. The design depends on how many teeth are missing, the condition of the remaining teeth and gums, and the support available.

There is more than one denture option.Partial dentures, complete dentures and implant-supported options may be considered in different situations. A dental assessment helps determine which options are realistic for you.

Types of tooth replacement that may be discussed

Partial denture

A removable appliance designed to replace some missing teeth while other natural teeth remain.

Complete denture

A removable option used when all teeth in an arch are missing.

Implant-supported option

Some patients may be assessed for implants to improve support or retention of a prosthesis.

Fit and maintenance matter

Dentures need regular cleaning and the tissues underneath them also need care. Changes in the mouth over time can affect fit, comfort and chewing, so professional review may be needed even when a denture has been used for years.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get used to dentures?

Adaptation varies between people and depends on the type of denture, fit and condition of the mouth.

Can dentures be repaired or adjusted?

Some fit or repair problems can be managed, but the denture and mouth need to be assessed first.

Are implants better than dentures?

They are different options with different requirements. Implant suitability, cost, maintenance and anatomy all influence the choice.
