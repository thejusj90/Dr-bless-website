Urgent dental care in Goa

Dental problems that should not wait

Severe tooth pain, dental trauma, a broken tooth, swelling or an infection may need prompt assessment. The appropriate treatment depends on the cause.

Is this a medical emergency?Difficulty breathing or swallowing, spreading facial or neck swelling, uncontrolled bleeding, or serious facial trauma should be treated as an emergency and may require immediate hospital care.

Common urgent dental problems

Severe tooth pain

Pain that is strong, persistent or affecting sleep should be assessed promptly.

Swelling

Dental or facial swelling can be associated with infection and should not be ignored.

Broken or knocked tooth

Trauma can damage the tooth, supporting tissues or jaw and may need same-day assessment.

What to do before you arrive

  • Contact the clinic and describe the problem clearly.
  • For a knocked-out permanent tooth, handle it by the crown rather than the root and seek urgent dental advice.
  • Avoid placing aspirin or other chemicals directly on the gum or tooth.
  • If there is uncontrolled bleeding or breathing/swallowing difficulty, seek emergency medical care.

Frequently asked questions

Can tooth pain be treated without knowing the cause?

Safe treatment depends on diagnosis, so the tooth and surrounding tissues need to be assessed.

Can swelling wait until tomorrow?

Some swelling can progress. Rapidly increasing swelling, fever, difficulty swallowing or breathing needs urgent attention.

Can a broken tooth be saved?

Sometimes, depending on the fracture, remaining tooth structure and supporting tissues.
