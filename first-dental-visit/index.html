What to expect at your first dental appointment

A first visit is usually about understanding your concerns, reviewing your dental and medical history, examining your mouth and agreeing on the next step.

What should I bring?Bring information about relevant medical conditions and medicines, previous dental records or X-rays if available, and a clear description of the problem you want addressed.

A typical first appointment may include

  • Discussion of your main concern and dental history.
  • Review of relevant medical conditions and medications.
  • Examination of teeth, gums, bite and other oral tissues.
  • Dental imaging when clinically required.
  • Discussion of findings, priorities and treatment options.

If you are nervous about dental treatment

Tell the clinic when booking. Knowing what makes you anxious can help the team explain each step and plan the visit at a comfortable pace.

Questions worth asking

What is the diagnosis?

Ask what the dentist believes is causing the problem and what evidence supports that conclusion.

What are my options?

For many dental problems there may be more than one reasonable treatment approach.

What happens next?

Ask about urgency, number of visits, maintenance and expected follow-up.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an X-ray at the first visit?

Not always. Imaging is advised when it is needed to answer a clinical question or plan treatment.
