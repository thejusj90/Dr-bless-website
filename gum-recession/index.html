Gum recession in Goa

Receding gums and exposed tooth roots

Gum recession means the gum margin has moved away from its previous position, making the tooth appear longer or exposing part of the root.

Can receding gums grow back?The gum does not simply regrow in every case. Management depends on the cause, severity, symptoms and condition of the supporting tissues.

Why recession may occur

Periodontal disease

Loss of supporting tissues can change the position of the gum margin.

Toothbrush trauma

Overly forceful brushing can contribute to recession in susceptible areas.

Tooth position and thin tissues

Anatomy, tooth position and a thin gum type can influence recession risk.

What an assessment may include

  • Measure the recession and gum attachment.
  • Check for inflammation and plaque.
  • Review brushing technique and local risk factors.
  • Assess sensitivity, root wear and tooth position.
  • Discuss monitoring, non-surgical care or periodontal procedures where appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

Can gum recession cause sensitivity?

Yes. Exposed root surfaces can be sensitive to cold, touch or acidic foods.

Does every case need surgery?

No. Some cases are monitored or managed by controlling the cause and protecting the root surface.

Who evaluates gum recession?

A dentist can assess recession; a periodontist has advanced training focused on gums and supporting tissues.
