Reasons to consider a gum evaluation

Bleeding gums

Persistent bleeding while brushing or flossing can be a reason for professional evaluation.

Gum recession

Receding gums or exposed roots may require assessment of the supporting tissues.

Loose or shifting teeth

Changes in tooth mobility or position should be evaluated clinically.

What a periodontal appointment may include

  • Review of symptoms and relevant dental history.
  • Examination of gum inflammation, recession, plaque and calculus.
  • Periodontal measurements where appropriate.
  • Dental imaging when needed to evaluate supporting structures.
  • A personalised plan for treatment and maintenance.

Frequently asked questions

Why do gums bleed?

Bleeding can have different causes. A dental examination helps determine whether gum inflammation, local irritation or another factor is involved.

Is a routine cleaning the same as periodontal treatment?

No. A routine cleaning and treatment for deeper periodontal problems are different and are selected according to clinical findings.

How often should gum health be reviewed?

The interval varies depending on current gum condition, disease history, home care and individual risk factors.
