Reasons to book a gum evaluation

Bleeding gums

Repeated bleeding during brushing or flossing deserves professional evaluation.

Gum recession

Exposed roots or a visibly receding gum line can have different causes and may need periodontal assessment.

Loose or shifting teeth

Changes in mobility or position can be associated with loss of support around teeth and should be examined.

What a periodontal appointment may include

  • Discussion of symptoms, oral-health habits and relevant dental history.
  • Examination for inflammation, plaque, calculus, recession and gum condition.
  • Periodontal measurements where appropriate.
  • Dental imaging when needed to assess supporting structures.
  • A treatment and maintenance plan based on the findings.

Routine cleaning versus periodontal treatment

A routine professional cleaning and treatment for established periodontal problems are not necessarily the same. The depth and type of treatment depend on the condition of the gums and supporting structures. An examination helps determine which level of care is appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

Why do my gums bleed?

Bleeding can be associated with gum inflammation and other local factors. A dental examination is needed to determine the cause in your case.

Can gum disease affect dental implants?

The tissues around implants also require ongoing hygiene and professional maintenance. Periodontal health is an important part of implant care.

How often should gum health be reviewed?

Review intervals vary depending on gum condition, disease history, home care and individual risk factors.
