Missing tooth options in Goa

What can replace a missing tooth?

A missing tooth can sometimes be replaced with a dental implant, bridge or removable denture. The best option depends on the location of the gap, neighbouring teeth, gums, bone, bite and your preferences.

There is no single replacement that is right for everyone.An assessment looks at the whole mouth and the missing-tooth area before comparing suitable options.

Common replacement options

Dental implant

An implant-supported tooth may be considered when the site, gums, bone and health factors are suitable.

Dental bridge

A bridge uses neighbouring teeth or other support to replace a missing tooth or teeth.

Removable denture

A partial or complete denture may replace one or several missing teeth depending on the situation.

What affects the choice?

  • How many teeth are missing and where the gap is located.
  • Health and structure of neighbouring teeth.
  • Condition of the gums and supporting bone.
  • Your bite, oral hygiene and maintenance needs.
  • Medical history, treatment time, preferences and budget.
