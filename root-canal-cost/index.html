Root canal cost in Goa

What affects the cost of root canal treatment?

Root canal fees can vary because teeth differ in anatomy, the severity of infection, previous treatment and the restoration needed afterward.

Why is diagnosis important before pricing?Tooth pain does not always require root canal treatment. The dentist first needs to confirm the diagnosis and determine the complexity of the tooth.

Factors that can affect the fee

Type of tooth

Front teeth, premolars and molars can differ in the number and complexity of root canals.

Treatment complexity

Calcified canals, unusual anatomy, previous treatment or significant infection can make treatment more complex.

Final restoration

A filling, core build-up or crown may be recommended after treatment depending on the remaining tooth structure.

What to ask when comparing prices

  • Is the consultation or imaging included?
  • Does the fee cover the complete root canal procedure?
  • Is the final filling or crown included or separate?
  • Are additional visits or retreatment considerations explained?

Frequently asked questions

Can you quote an exact root canal price before examining the tooth?

An accurate treatment plan generally requires examination and, when indicated, dental imaging.

Does every root canal tooth need a crown?

No. The need for a crown depends on tooth position and the amount of remaining tooth structure.

Is extraction cheaper than root canal treatment?

The immediate cost can differ, but the comparison should also consider whether the tooth can be retained and the future cost of replacing a missing tooth.
