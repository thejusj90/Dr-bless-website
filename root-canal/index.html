Root canal treatment in Margao, Goa

Root canal treatment for a damaged or painful tooth

A root canal may be recommended after a dentist examines the tooth and confirms that the tissue inside it is affected. The goal is to retain the natural tooth when that is clinically appropriate.

Start with an assessmentTooth pain does not always mean a root canal is required. The clinic first evaluates the tooth and discusses the available treatment options.

When a dental assessment may be useful

Persistent tooth pain

Ongoing pain is a reason to have the tooth examined.

Deep decay

A heavily decayed tooth may require further evaluation before a treatment plan is selected.

Damaged tooth

A cracked, broken or previously treated tooth may need assessment of the remaining tooth structure.

What the consultation covers

  • Your symptoms and dental history.
  • Clinical examination of the tooth and surrounding tissues.
  • Dental imaging when required.
  • Discussion of treatment options and the final restoration if treatment is recommended.

Frequently asked questions

Does every toothache need a root canal?

No. Different dental problems can cause tooth pain, so diagnosis comes first.

Can a treated tooth still need a crown?

Sometimes. The need for a crown or another restoration depends on the condition and location of the tooth.

How do I know which option is right?

The dentist discusses suitable options after examining the tooth and reviewing any required imaging.
