Sensitive teeth in Goa

Why are my teeth sensitive?

Sensitivity to cold, heat, sweets, air or brushing can have several causes. Exposed root surfaces, worn enamel, decay, cracks, gum recession and recent dental treatment are among the possibilities a dentist may consider.

Persistent sensitivity deserves an assessment.Short, occasional sensitivity and sharp pain from a specific tooth are not always the same problem. A clinical examination helps identify the source.

What may cause tooth sensitivity?

Gum recession

When the gum line recedes, root surfaces may become exposed and more sensitive.

Tooth wear or decay

Loss of protective tooth structure or cavities can increase sensitivity.

Cracks or dental treatment

A cracked tooth or recent procedure can sometimes produce temporary or persistent sensitivity.

Why the location of the sensitivity matters

Generalised sensitivity across several teeth may have different causes from sharp pain coming from one specific tooth. The pattern, duration and triggers help guide the dental examination.
