Swollen gums in Goa

Swollen gums can signal inflammation or infection

Gum swelling can occur around one tooth or across several areas. Persistent swelling, bleeding, pain, bad taste or pus should be professionally evaluated.

When is gum swelling urgent?If swelling is rapidly increasing, spreading to the face or neck, or accompanied by fever, difficulty swallowing or breathing, seek urgent medical or dental care.

Common dental reasons for swollen gums

Plaque-related inflammation

Plaque accumulation along the gumline can trigger redness, swelling and bleeding.

Local infection

An infection around a tooth or gum pocket can cause a tender, localised swelling.

Irritation around a tooth

Food trapping, an erupting wisdom tooth or a local dental problem may inflame the gum.

What the dentist may check

  • Location and pattern of swelling.
  • Bleeding, tenderness and gum pocketing.
  • Condition of nearby teeth and restorations.
  • Dental imaging when needed to identify the source.

Frequently asked questions

Can salt-water rinses cure swollen gums?

A gentle rinse may temporarily soothe some irritation, but persistent swelling still needs evaluation of the cause.

Should I stop brushing swollen gums?

Keeping the area clean is generally important, but brush gently and seek advice if the area is very painful or bleeding heavily.

Can swollen gums be related to a tooth infection?

Yes. Gum swelling can sometimes be associated with an underlying tooth or periodontal infection.
