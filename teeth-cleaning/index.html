Why professional cleaning may be recommended

Plaque and calculus

Hardened calculus cannot be removed effectively with normal brushing and may require professional scaling.

Gum inflammation

Redness, swelling or bleeding may indicate that the gums need closer evaluation.

Preventive maintenance

Regular professional review can support daily brushing, flossing and long-term oral care.

Cleaning versus gum treatment

A routine cleaning is not the same as treatment for established periodontal disease. If deeper gum problems are present, the treatment plan may be different. The correct approach depends on examination findings.

Frequently asked questions

How often should teeth be professionally cleaned?

The interval varies according to gum health, plaque build-up, previous dental problems and individual risk factors.

Will cleaning whiten my teeth?

Cleaning can remove some surface deposits and stains, but professional whitening is a separate cosmetic treatment.

What if my gums bleed during brushing?

Persistent bleeding is a reason to have the gums evaluated rather than assuming a routine cleaning alone is enough.
