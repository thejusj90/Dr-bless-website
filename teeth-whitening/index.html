Teeth whitening in Goa

Professional options for a brighter smile

Teeth whitening can improve the appearance of some types of tooth discoloration. An oral-health check is useful first to identify whether whitening is appropriate for your teeth and gums.

Whitening is not the same as cleaning.Cleaning removes plaque, calculus and some surface stains. Whitening is a separate cosmetic procedure intended to lighten the appearance of natural tooth structure.

What is checked before whitening?

Tooth and gum health

Active decay, gum problems or sensitivity may need attention before cosmetic whitening is considered.

Type of discoloration

Not all discoloration responds in the same way, so the likely result varies from person to person.

Existing restorations

Crowns, veneers and tooth-coloured fillings do not necessarily change shade in the same way as natural teeth.

What about sensitivity?

Some people experience temporary sensitivity during or after whitening. Your dentist can assess existing sensitivity and discuss whether whitening is suitable and how to manage the procedure.

Frequently asked questions

How white will my teeth become?

Results vary with the starting shade, type of discoloration and the individual response of the teeth.

Does whitening work on crowns or veneers?

Dental restorations generally do not respond like natural tooth enamel, so colour matching may need separate planning.

Should I clean my teeth before whitening?

The dentist may recommend professional cleaning first if plaque, calculus or surface staining is present.
