Tooth extraction in Goa

When a tooth may need to be removed

Dentists generally assess whether a tooth can be retained before recommending extraction. Removal may be considered when a tooth is severely damaged, infected, poorly positioned or cannot be predictably restored.

Assessment comes before extraction.The dentist examines the tooth, surrounding gums and relevant dental history. Imaging may be required depending on the tooth and its position.

Reasons extraction may be discussed

Severely damaged tooth

A tooth with insufficient remaining structure may not always be suitable for predictable restoration.

Infection or pain

When a tooth cannot be retained appropriately, extraction may be considered after diagnosis.

Position or crowding

Some extractions may form part of a broader orthodontic or dental treatment plan.

What happens after a tooth is removed?

Aftercare depends on the tooth and procedure. If the extracted tooth needs replacement, options such as an implant, bridge or removable denture may be discussed once the clinical situation is understood.

Frequently asked questions

Can every painful tooth be saved?

No. Whether a tooth can be retained depends on the cause of the problem and how much healthy tooth and supporting structure remain.

Do I need an X-ray before extraction?

Dental imaging may be advised depending on the tooth, symptoms and clinical findings.

Should a missing tooth always be replaced?

Not necessarily, but replacement options and the effects of leaving the space should be discussed for your individual case.
