Tooth pain in Goa

Toothache needs a diagnosis first

Tooth pain can come from decay, cracks, infection, sensitivity, gum problems, bite-related issues or other causes. The treatment depends on identifying the source of the pain.

Does tooth pain always mean a root canal?No. A root canal is only one possible treatment. A dentist first examines the tooth and may use dental imaging or other tests when needed.

What your dentist may look for

Decay

Cavities can range from shallow damage to deeper involvement of the inner tooth tissues.

Cracks or fractures

A damaged tooth may hurt during chewing, temperature changes or spontaneously.

Gum or supporting-tissue problems

Pain around a tooth can sometimes come from the gums or tissues surrounding it rather than the tooth itself.

When should you seek prompt dental care?

Persistent or worsening pain, swelling, difficulty chewing, a broken tooth or pain that disrupts normal activity are reasons to arrange a dental assessment. If you feel seriously unwell or have rapidly increasing facial swelling or difficulty breathing or swallowing, seek urgent medical care.

Possible next steps after diagnosis

Depending on the cause, treatment may range from preventive care or a filling to root canal treatment, gum care, restoration or extraction. The correct choice depends on whether the tooth can be predictably restored and the condition of the surrounding tissues.
