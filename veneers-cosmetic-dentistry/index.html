Cosmetic dentistry in Goa

Veneers and cosmetic dentistry for smile concerns

Cosmetic dentistry can address concerns involving tooth colour, shape, proportion, spacing and visible restorations. The best option depends on the condition of the teeth and gums.

Common reasons people ask about cosmetic dentistry

Colour concerns

Professional whitening or restorative options may be discussed depending on the cause of discolouration.

Shape or chips

Bonding, veneers or other restorations may be considered after assessing the tooth structure.

Spacing or alignment

Some concerns may be better addressed with orthodontic treatment rather than restorations.

What are veneers?

Veneers are thin restorations placed on the visible surface of teeth in selected cases. They are not appropriate for every cosmetic concern and may involve alteration of tooth structure.

Treatment planning

  • Evaluate teeth, gums and bite.
  • Clarify the change you want to achieve.
  • Discuss conservative and restorative options.
  • Review limitations, maintenance and alternatives before treatment.

Frequently asked questions

Are veneers reversible?

Many veneer treatments require some tooth preparation, so they should be considered carefully after a clinical consultation.

Should gum problems be treated before cosmetic dentistry?

Active gum disease should be evaluated and managed before elective cosmetic treatment.

Are clear aligners a cosmetic option?

They can be considered when tooth position or spacing is the main concern, subject to orthodontic assessment.
