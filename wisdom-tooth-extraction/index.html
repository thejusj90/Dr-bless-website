Wisdom tooth care in Goa

Wisdom tooth assessment for pain, swelling or impaction

Wisdom teeth do not automatically need removal. Extraction may be considered when a wisdom tooth is causing repeated problems or is unlikely to remain healthy.

When should I have a wisdom tooth checked?Persistent pain, swelling, difficulty cleaning the area, repeated infection or damage to a nearby tooth are reasons to arrange an assessment.

What the dentist evaluates

  • Position and eruption of the wisdom tooth.
  • Condition of the surrounding gum and nearby tooth.
  • Symptoms such as pain, swelling or recurrent infection.
  • Dental imaging when required to understand the tooth position and nearby structures.

Possible treatment decisions

Monitor

A healthy, symptom-free wisdom tooth may simply be reviewed over time.

Treat surrounding inflammation

Short-term local care may be needed when the gum around a partially erupted tooth is inflamed.

Extraction

Removal may be advised when the expected benefit is greater than the risks of keeping the tooth.

Frequently asked questions

Does every impacted wisdom tooth need removal?

No. The decision depends on symptoms, disease risk, tooth position and clinical findings.

Will I need an X-ray?

Imaging is commonly used when it is important to assess the wisdom tooth and nearby anatomy.

What if my face is very swollen?

Rapidly increasing swelling, fever, difficulty swallowing or breathing needs urgent medical or dental attention.
